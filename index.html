01 About

About

I'm a computer engineer with an MBA, working as a Professional Services Consultant at Esker. I implement SaaS solutions that automate Procure-to-Pay and Order-to-Cash processes and integrate them with ERP systems such as SAP and Oracle, from client workshops and functional specifications to go-live and support.

Earlier, I worked as an IT consultant at PwC on IT audit, risk and cybersecurity engagements and on data analysis. In parallel, I design and build my own iOS and Android apps and AI-assisted tools.

I've lived and worked in Madrid and Dublin, and I'm now based in Paris. I work with clients in Spanish, English and French. Sport has always been part of my life: I played semi-professional basketball in the Real Madrid youth academy until I was 16, and I'm now an amateur triathlete and a fan of any endurance sport.
